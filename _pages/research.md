## Research interests

Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning and Information Retrieval. Specific topics:

- Neural Language Models
- Text Mining
- Knowledge Extraction
- Knowledge Graphs
- Semantic Information Retrieval
- Geographic Information Retrieval (GIR)
- Word Sense Disambiguation
- Automatic detection of Irony/Humour in text
- Sentiment Analysis and Opinion Mining
- Securisation of Large Language Models: preventing memorization of sensitive information
- Graph Neural Networks and applications to Knowledge Graphs and Knowledge Extraction

See also: [publications](/publications/), [projects](/research/projects/), [events and invited talks](/research/events/).
