## Information

This course is in English. Part of the INF582 module in collaboration with Michalis Vazirgiannis.

Syllabus (for my part of the course):

- Lexical statistics and language models (n-gram models; neural language models)
- Sequence labelling: Named Entity Recognition, Part Of Speech tagging using Hidden Markov Models and Conditional Random Fields
- Knowledge and Knowledge Representation: Knowledge Graph embeddings (TransE and similar methods)
- Modern LLM problems: detection of generated text, privacy issues and hallucinations

Note: all material is available on the IP Paris Moodle; this page is just for reference.

## Additional material

- D. Jurafsky and H. Martin: Speech and Language Processing ([draft](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/) available online)
- Y. Goldberg: Neural Network Methods for Natural Language Processing
