I am a Maître de Conférences (Associate Professor) HDR at Université Sorbonne Paris Nord, and Professeur Chargé de Cours (part-time Lecturer) at DIX, École Polytechnique.

  • Member of the Laboratoire d’Informatique de Paris Nord (LIPN), Knowledge Representation and Natural Language (RCLN) team
  • Guest member of the DaSciM team, LIX
  • Member of InidEx EFL Workgroup 4
  • Scientific Communications Coordinator of COST Action 24121 (KGELL)
